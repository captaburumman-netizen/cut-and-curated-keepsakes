Hi, I'm so glad you're here.

Every keepsake starts
with a story.

I'm the maker behind Cut & Curated Keepsakes — a one-woman creative studio nestled in Edmond, Oklahoma. What started as a passion for making things beautiful and meaningful has grown into a small business built on one belief: the best gifts are the ones that feel personal.

Each piece I make is crafted by hand, one at a time, using resin, vinyl, pressed botanicals, and a whole lot of love. Whether it's a birth-stat plaque for a new baby or a curated gift basket for Eid, I pour everything I have into it — because you deserve something truly special.
