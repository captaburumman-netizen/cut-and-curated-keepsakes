About Cut & Curated

Hi, I'm [Your Name]!

Every piece starts with a person.

I started Cut & Curated Keepsakes because I believe the gifts that matter most are the ones made for you — not pulled off a shelf, but thought about, crafted, and created with a specific person in mind.

What started as a hobby — pouring resin at my kitchen table late at night — turned into something I couldn't stop doing. Every time I saw someone's face when they received a personalized piece, I knew this was more than a hobby. It was a calling.

I'm based in Edmond, Oklahoma, and I make every single item by hand. That means I'm involved in every step — from choosing the right colors and inclusions, to writing the name just right, to wrapping it up in tissue paper before it leaves my door. There's no assembly line here. Just me, my craft supplies, and a whole lot of love.

Thank you for supporting a small business. It genuinely means everything.
