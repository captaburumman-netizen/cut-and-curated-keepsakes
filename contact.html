Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an order take?

Processing times vary by product and are listed on each product page — typically 1–10 business days. Custom orders may take longer; we'll discuss timelines before you commit.

Do you offer local pickup?

Yes! Free local pickup is available in the Edmond, Oklahoma area. Select "Local Pickup" at checkout and I'll contact you to arrange a time and place.

Can I request changes after ordering?

Contact me as soon as possible at hello@cutandcuratedks.com. If I haven't started your piece yet, changes are usually possible. Once work has begun, changes may not be possible.

What's your return policy?

Because every item is made to order and personalized, I generally cannot accept returns. However, if your item arrives damaged or is significantly different from what was ordered, please reach out right away and I'll make it right.

Do you ship outside of Oklahoma?

Yes! I ship anywhere in the United States via USPS. Shipping options and rates are shown at checkout.
